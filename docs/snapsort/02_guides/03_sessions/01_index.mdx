## Every Drag Is a Session

A `DragSession` is the temporary state SnapSort creates for one drag gesture.
One internal controller on the root Container follows the pointer, resolves
prospective destinations, owns temporary ghosts, commits the result, and
cleans everything up.

The root's lifecycle callbacks and `root.dragSession` expose the same stable
public session handle while the gesture is live.

Locations carried by an event describe the direct source, prospective target,
or final destination. They do not necessarily identify the Container whose
callback is running. Structural and root lifecycle callbacks run on the root;
drop policy, geometry, and item-hover callbacks run on the direct Container
whose candidate or Item they inspect.

## Terms

- **Root / DragSession:** the top-level Container and its public session
  handle. SnapSort's internal controller orchestrates target resolution,
  ghost relocation, commits, and cleanup.
- **Source Container:** an Item's direct parent when the drag begins. In the
  diagram this lane also represents the current owner for operations such as
  ghost or Item removal.
- **Target Container:** the prospective direct Container and index selected
  while the pointer moves.
- **Destination Container:** the final direct Container and index reported
  when the drag ends. It may be the source again, or `null` when the lifecycle
  ends without a resolved destination.
- **Ghost Item:** passive temporary render state managed by the session. A
  ghost does not ask to insert or remove itself.
