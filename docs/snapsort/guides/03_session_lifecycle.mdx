When an item is dragged, SnapSort creates one internal session controller for
the root container. It exposes a stable, read-only `DragSession` handle through
callbacks and `root.dragSession`, follows the pointer, resolves prospective
destinations, owns temporary ghosts, and invokes callbacks on the container
responsible for each operation.

## Terms

- **Root / DragSession:** the top-level container and its public session handle.
  SnapSort's internal controller orchestrates target resolution, ghost
  relocation, commits, and cleanup.
- **Source Container:** an item's direct parent when the drag begins. In the
  diagram this lane also represents the current owner for operations such as
  ghost or item removal.
- **Target Container:** the prospective direct container and index selected while the
  pointer moves.
- **Destination Container:** the final direct container and index reported when the drag
  ends. It may be the source again, or `null` when the lifecycle ends without a
  resolved destination.
- **Ghost Item:** passive placeholder data managed by the lifecycle. A ghost
  does not ask to insert or remove itself.
